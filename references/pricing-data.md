# Pricing Data — Known Data Points

Last updated: 2026-03-29
Sources verified by web search and web fetch.

## Sample Acquisition

### Government Biobanks

**NIA Aging Research Biobank** (US)
- Pulling vials: $7.91 per vial shipped
- Pulling + aliquoting: $9.80 per vial shipped
- Custom nucleic acid: $29.09 per vial shipped
- Data query support: free
- Source: https://agingresearchbiobank.nia.nih.gov/costs/
- Verified: 2026-03-29
- Confidence: PUBLISHED
- Notes: Payment required before shipment. USD only, check only. Researcher provides shipper account (FedEx/UPS).

### Hospital/Academic Biobanks (US)

**Boston Medical Center LABS Core**
- Plasma (cohort): $15 per 0.5mL or 1.0mL aliquot
- Serum (cohort): $24 per 0.75mL aliquot
- Clinical remnant plasma/serum: $12 whole sample, $24 aliquoted
- PBMCs: $29 per aliquot
- Buffy coat: $43 per sample
- Processing (serum/plasma): $24 per sample
- Storage: $1 per box per month (box holds 100 cryotubes)
- Sample intake fee: $3 per sample
- Source: https://www.bmc.org/research/labs-core/fees
- Verified: 2026-03-29
- Confidence: PUBLISHED
- Notes: Cost-neutral to hospital. External party fees available on request.

**USA Health Biobank (University of South Alabama)**
- Blood/urine/fluid procurement: $10 per sample
- Tissue procurement: $15 per sample
- Fresh tissue procurement: $35 per sample
- Plasma/serum processing: $8 per sample
- PBMC processing: $30 per sample
- Dissemination (stored blood/plasma): $8 per unit
- Dissemination (fresh blood): $10 per unit
- Dissemination (stored tissue): $15 per unit
- Dissemination (fresh tissue): $30 per unit
- Storage: $20 per month (up to 2 years, then released to general inventory)
- Retrieval: free ($15 with QC assessment)
- Source: https://www.southalabama.edu/colleges/com/research/biobank-histology/services.html
- Verified: 2026-03-29
- Confidence: PUBLISHED

### Population Biobanks (Europe)

**Lifelines Biobank (Netherlands, 167K participants)**
- 500 plasma/serum/urine samples: starting EUR 9,500 (EUR 19/sample)
- 1,000-3,000 samples: ~EUR 16,000 (EUR 5-16/sample)
- 10,000+ samples: ~EUR 37,000 (EUR 3.70/sample)
- DNA samples 500: ~EUR 14,000 (EUR 28/sample)
- DNA samples 2,000: ~EUR 32,000 (EUR 16/sample)
- DNA extraction from buffy coat 500: ~EUR 23,000 (EUR 46/sample)
- Data access required: EUR 4,500/year per workspace user
- Shipping NL: EUR 50/box (~770 samples)
- Shipping EU: EUR 300/box
- Source: https://www.lifelines-biobank.com/researchers/what-we-offer/biosamples/costs-of-biosamples
- Verified: 2026-03-29
- Confidence: PUBLISHED
- Notes: Non-profit, cost-recovery pricing. Prices exclude VAT. Analysis methods not included.

### Open Research Cohorts

**ADNI (Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative)**
- Data access: free via LONI portal (https://ida.loni.usc.edu)
- Requirements: DUA signature
- Physical biospecimen requests: managed by RARC and NIA
- Commercial use: allowed (public-private partnership)
- Source: https://adni.loni.usc.edu/data-samples/
- Verified: 2026-03-29
- Confidence: PUBLISHED

**UK Biobank**
- Data access: GBP 9,000 + VAT for 3 years (UK-based researchers)
- Students/LMIC: GBP 500 for 3 years
- Extension: GBP 175 per year
- Compute costs: additional (DNAnexus RAP platform)
- Commercial orgs: explicitly allowed for "health-related research in public interest"
- Insurance companies: explicitly banned
- Source: https://www.ukbiobank.ac.uk/use-our-data/fees/
- Verified: 2026-03-29
- Confidence: PUBLISHED

**NACC (National Alzheimer's Coordinating Center)**
- Data access: free to all researchers
- Requirements: proposal with research hypothesis + DUA
- No academic/commercial distinction
- Source: https://naccdata.org/requesting-data/nacc-data/
- Verified: 2026-03-29
- Confidence: PUBLISHED

## Assay/Platform Costs

### Academic Core Facilities

**EMBL Metabolomics Core (Europe, 2024 prices)**
- Untargeted metabolomics (academic EMBL member): EUR 34-43/sample (volume dependent)
- Untargeted metabolomics (non-academic/non-member): EUR 105-130/sample
- Untargeted lipidomics (academic): EUR 40-49/sample
- Untargeted lipidomics (non-academic): EUR 120-150/sample
- Instrument hour rate: EUR 102
- Sample prep extra: EUR 10/sample (monophasic), EUR 15/sample (biphasic)
- Source: https://www.embl.org/groups/metabolomics/service-fees-external-users/
- Verified: 2026-03-29 (valid from 01.08.2024)
- Confidence: PUBLISHED
- Notes: Per ionization mode. Most analyses need pos+neg = 2x cost. 4-6 samples minimum per condition.

**Baylor College of Medicine Metabolomics Core**
- Untargeted metabolomics: $300/sample
- Untargeted lipidomics: $273/sample
- Targeted assays (amino acids, carnitines, bile acids, etc.): $45-85 per 10 samples for serum/plasma
- Metabolic flux (isotope tracing): $2,047.50 per 15 samples
- Source: https://www.bcm.edu/research/atc-core-labs/metabolomics-core/services-and-fees
- Verified: 2026-03-29
- Confidence: PUBLISHED
- Notes: Internal BCM + MoU institutions. Additional cost for absolute quantification.

**Duke University Proteomics & Metabolomics Core (2024 prices)**
- Biocrates MxP Quant 500: $90/sample (Duke), $145/sample (non-Duke) + kit
- Biocrates AbsoluteIDQ p180: $62 (Duke), $100 (non-Duke) + kit
- High-res lipid profiling (ESI+ and ESI-): $321 (Duke), $517 (non-Duke)
- Fast HR metabolite profiling (one mode): $177 (Duke), $285 (non-Duke)
- Bile acids panel (Biocrates kit): $43 (Duke), $69 (non-Duke) + kit
- Acylcarnitines: $30 (Duke), $48 (non-Duke)
- Source: https://medschool.duke.edu/research/research-support/core-facilities-service-centers/find-service-center/proteomics-and-0
- Verified: 2026-03-29 (effective July 1, 2024)
- Confidence: PUBLISHED
- Notes: Kit costs from Biocrates are additional. External academic and industry rates differ.

**General Metabolics**

## Gaps (no public pricing available)

- **Commercial biobanks**: BioIVT, Discovery Life Sciences, Precision for Medicine, iSpecimen, Crown Bioscience, REPROCELL Bioserve — all quote-based
- **Metabolon**: private, no public pricing
- **Hospital-to-buyer intermediaries**: ABS Bio, BIOMEDICA CRO — quote-based
- **BBMRI-ERIC member biobanks**: each sets own fees, no central pricing
- **Legal/DUA overhead**: highly variable ($5K-50K per agreement), institution-dependent
- **NIH grant metabolomics line items**: total budgets public via RePORTER, but metabolomics-specific allocations require reading individual applications
